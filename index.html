Signal Desk

Buy, stop and sell levels for Binance spot, from one fixed rule. It reads public prices and places no orders.

The rule: buy when a candle closes above the highest price of the last 20 candles and above the 50-EMA, while the 50-EMA is above the 200-EMA. Stop 3 ATR under that close. Sell when a candle closes under the 50-EMA.

These are levels from a fixed rule, not forecasts, and not financial advice. You can lose money on any trade. Your settings and trades are stored only in this browser.
